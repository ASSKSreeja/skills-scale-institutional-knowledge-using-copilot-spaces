# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Key Interactions
- Works with QA/Testing Lead on acceptance criteria and test coverage
- Collaborates with Technical Architect/Lead on design decisions
- Partners with DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer on deployment concerns
- Engages with Security Engineer on vulnerability assessment

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Key Interactions
- Aligns with Stakeholder/Sponsor on business priorities
- Works with Designer/UX Lead on user experience strategy
- Partners with Project Manager on execution planning
- Engages with QA/Testing Lead on acceptance criteria

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Key Interactions
- Coordinates with Scrum Master/Agile Coach on team ceremonies
- Escalates risks and blockers to Stakeholder/Sponsor
- Works with all roles to maintain project timeline
- Partners with Technical Architect/Lead on dependency identification

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define and execute quality assurance strategies, ensuring all deliverables meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans and strategies aligned with project scope
- Define acceptance criteria with Product Manager and Developers
- Execute manual and automated testing
- Identify, document, and track defects
- Validate fixes and coordinate regression testing
- Recommend quality improvements and test automation opportunities
- Ensure compliance and security testing coverage

### Goals
- Catch issues early and reduce production defects
- Enable confident releases through comprehensive testing
- Maintain high quality standards across all deliverables
- Build repeatable, scalable testing processes

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and testing estimation
- Defect reports and quality dashboards
- Test coverage and release readiness assessments
- Collaboration with Developers on test automation

### Key Interactions
- Partners with Developers on test case design and automation
- Aligns with Product Manager on acceptance criteria
- Works with Security Engineer on security testing
- Coordinates with DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer on testing environments

---

## Technical Architect/Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects/Leads provide technical guidance, design decisions, and strategic oversight. They identify technical risks, evaluate trade-offs, and ensure solutions align with long-term system goals.

### Responsibilities
- Evaluate architectural approaches and design patterns
- Conduct technical design reviews with the team
- Identify technical dependencies and integration points
- Assess scalability, performance, and reliability concerns
- Mentor developers on best practices and design principles
- Provide technical risk assessment and mitigation strategies
- Collaborate on vendor/technology evaluations

### Goals
- Ensure technically sound, scalable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Build systems that are maintainable and resilient
- Accelerate decision-making through expert guidance

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Design documentation and decision records
- Technical risk assessments
- Mentoring and code review feedback

### Key Interactions
- Reviews designs with Developers and provides guidance
- Partners with Security Engineer on architectural security
- Works with DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer on deployment architecture
- Collaborates with Project Manager on dependency identification

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters/Agile Coaches facilitate team processes, remove impediments, and drive continuous improvement. They ensure the team operates effectively within agreed ceremonies and practices.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve team impediments and blockers
- Coach team members on Agile practices and mindsets
- Track metrics (velocity, burndown) and identify trends
- Foster psychological safety and encourage feedback
- Remove process bottlenecks and protect team focus time
- Support Project Manager in maintaining team health

### Goals
- Enable the team to deliver consistently
- Improve team velocity and predictability
- Build a high-performing, self-organizing team
- Foster continuous learning and improvement

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Coaching conversations and impediment resolution
- Team health and velocity metrics

### Key Interactions
- Works with Project Manager on schedule coordination
- Supports Developers by removing blockers
- Helps Product Manager prioritize backlog work
- Facilitates communication across all roles

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders/Sponsors provide strategic direction, business context, and executive oversight. They approve scope, prioritize initiatives, and remove business-level obstacles.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria
- Approve project scope and major changes
- Prioritize projects and resolve business trade-offs
- Provide or secure necessary resources and budget
- Communicate project status to leadership
- Remove business-level obstacles and approvals
- Make go/no-go decisions at key gates

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Align project execution with business strategy
- Maintain stakeholder confidence and communication
- Make informed decisions with clear trade-off understanding

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval meetings
- Monthly or milestone-based status updates
- Go/no-go decision gates
- Escalations requiring executive intervention

### Key Interactions
- Reviews Project Charter with Product Manager
- Makes priority decisions in collaboration with Product Manager
- Receives status updates from Project Manager
- Escalates resources or policy decisions as needed

---

## Designer/UX Lead

### Role Summary
Designers/UX Leads create user-centered designs, ensure consistency and accessibility, and advocate for user needs throughout the development process.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and gather requirements
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Establish design systems and accessibility standards
- Collaborate on usability testing and user validation
- Iterate on designs based on feedback
- Ensure design consistency across features and platforms
- Review implementations for design compliance

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce user friction and increase adoption
- Build a cohesive design language and system
- Advocate for user needs in product decisions

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- User research findings and personas
- Design specifications and accessibility checklists
- Usability testing reports and recommendations

### Key Interactions
- Collaborates with Product Manager on user priorities
- Partners with Developers on implementation feasibility
- Works with QA/Testing Lead on usability testing
- Aligns with Stakeholder/Sponsor on design direction

---

## Security Engineer

### Role Summary
Security Engineers identify and mitigate security risks, ensure compliance with security standards, and embed security practices throughout the development lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct security threat assessments and code reviews
- Define security requirements and acceptance criteria
- Perform or coordinate security testing and vulnerability scanning
- Identify and prioritize security risks
- Provide security guidance on architecture and design
- Ensure compliance with security policies and standards
- Participate in incident response and security retrospectives

### Goals
- Prevent security vulnerabilities and breaches
- Embed security into the development process
- Maintain compliance with regulatory requirements
- Build secure-by-design solutions

### Typical Communication
- Security threat assessments and risk reports
- Code review feedback and vulnerability findings
- Security requirements and compliance checklist
- Incident response and post-mortem analysis

### Key Interactions
- Reviews designs with Technical Architect/Lead
- Partners with Developers on secure coding practices
- Works with QA/Testing Lead on security testing
- Collaborates with DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer on infrastructure security

---

## DevOps/Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps/Infrastructure Engineers manage deployment pipelines, infrastructure, and operational readiness. They enable efficient, reliable delivery and support production systems.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain deployment pipelines and CI/CD infrastructure
- Manage environments (development, staging, production)
- Configure monitoring, logging, and alerting systems
- Coordinate releases and manage rollback procedures
- Optimize infrastructure for performance and cost
- Support incident response and troubleshooting
- Document operational procedures and runbooks

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable deployments with minimal risk
- Maintain high availability and performance
- Reduce deployment friction and manual effort
- Support safe, observable production systems

### Typical Communication
- Deployment status and release coordination
- Infrastructure and monitoring dashboards
- Runbooks and operational procedures
- Incident response and post-mortem meetings

### Key Interactions
- Partners with Developers on deployment concerns
- Works with Technical Architect/Lead on infrastructure design
- Coordinates with Security Engineer on infrastructure security
- Collaborates with QA/Testing Lead on testing environments
